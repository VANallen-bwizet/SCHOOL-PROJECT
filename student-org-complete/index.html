Compare two organizations

Difference finds members of A who are not in B. Symmetric difference finds members belonging to either organization, excluding those who belong to both.

  • Subset (A ⊆ B): Every member of A also belongs to B.
  • Superset (A ⊇ B): A contains every member of B.
  • Equal sets: A and B have exactly the same members, so both checks are Yes.

Organization similarity and overlap rankings

Performance laboratory

List lookup vs hash-set lookup

Compare Array.includes and Set.has on populations of 100, 1,000, 10,000, and 50,000 simulated IDs. Each trial uses 4,000 fixed queries: half hits and half misses. Results are medians of five trials after a warmup, with trial order alternated.

The benchmark runs in a worker, keeping this window responsive.

Read the results fairly.

List lookup is O(n) per query; hash-set lookup is average O(1), with O(n) construction and additional memory. Tiny timings are sensitive to runtime noise. Lookup-only speedup excludes set construction; compare the build-plus-lookup column for a one-time workload. These results do not measure SQLite speed.
